[1] Install
  (1) Extract and keep the folder structure intact:
        crt-geom-deluxe.cgp
        passes/crt-geom-deluxe.cg
        passes/gaussx.cg
        passes/gaussy.cg
  (2) Drop the whole folder into FBNeo's shaders directory.
  (3) Set the video renderer to Direct3D 11 Blitter and load
      crt-geom-deluxe.cgp.
  (4) Tune the 30 parameters under Video -> Shader Parameters.

[2] Render Chain
  pass0 gaussx  ->  pass1 gaussy  ->  pass2 crt-geom-deluxe

  - gaussx + gaussy: separable Gaussian blur, used only as the
    halation source, never shown directly.
  - pass2 internal order: curvature/corners/overscan -> Lanczos2
    resample -> motion blur -> scanline boost -> halation -> mask
    -> phosphor -> saturation -> NTSC color -> black level
    -> exposure -> color temperature.

  'Current' is the value actually loaded by this cgp; 'Range/Step'
  are the hard limits of the parameter panel.

[3] Quick Reference
====================================================================================================

-- Shadow Mask -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Parameter            | Current | Range         | Step   | Effect
  --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  mask_type            |       1 | 1~24          | 1      | Shadow mask pattern, 24 options (see
                                                            section 5)
  aperture_strength    |    0.40 | 0~1           | 0.05   | Mask darkening; larger = more visible
                                                            gaps
  aperture_brightboost |    0.50 | 0~1           | 0.05   | Bright-line boost; restores brightness
                                                            lost to the mask

-- Geometry ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Parameter            | Current | Range         | Step   | Effect
  --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  curvature            |       1 | 0~1           | 1      | Curvature switch; 1 = spherical bend, 0
                                                            = flat
  R                    |     3.5 | 0.5~10        | 0.1    | Radius of curvature; smaller = more
                                                            bulge
  d                    |     2.0 | 0.1~10        | 0.1    | Viewing distance; larger = flatter look
  angle_x              |     0.0 | -1~1          | 0.01   | Horizontal tilt (screen yaw)
  angle_y              |     0.0 | -1~1          | 0.01   | Vertical tilt (screen pitch)
  cornersize           |    0.01 | 0~0.10        | 0.01   | Rounded corner radius; 0 = square frame
  cornersmooth         |    1000 | 100~2000      | 100    | Corner edge smoothness; raise if jagged
  overscan_x           |     1.0 | 0.8~1.2       | 0.005  | Horizontal overscan zoom; <1 leaves
                                                            borders
  overscan_y           |     1.0 | 0.8~1.2       | 0.005  | Vertical overscan zoom; same as above
  aspect_x             |     1.0 | 0.3~1.0       | 0.01   | Source horizontal aspect (pixel-ratio
                                                            fix)
  aspect_y             |    0.75 | 0.3~1.0       | 0.01   | Source vertical aspect; arcade 4:3 uses
                                                            1.0/0.75

-- Scanline / Grille -------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Parameter            | Current | Range         | Step   | Effect
  --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  CRTgamma             |    2.20 | 0.7~4.0       | 0.05   | Gamma of the simulated CRT (decode)
  monitorgamma         |     2.2 | 0.7~4.0       | 0.05   | Output display gamma (encode); equal =
                                                            neutral tone
  scanline_weight      |    0.30 | 0.1~0.5       | 0.01   | Scanline thickness and darkness
  geom_lum             |    0.85 | 0~1           | 0.01   | Scanline luminance lift; ~1 removes dark
                                                            lines
  interlace_detect     |     1.0 | 0~1           | 1      | Interlacing simulation switch
                                                            (interlaced sources)
  pixel_glow           |    0.30 | 0~1           | 0.05   | Pixel-center glow / edge softening; 0 =
                                                            sharp

-- Halation ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Parameter            | Current | Range         | Step   | Effect
  --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  halation             |    0.10 | 0~0.3         | 0.01   | Halation strength; bright areas bleed
                                                            out, 0 = off
  width                |    0.50 | 0.1~4.0       | 0.1    | Halation blur radius

-- Phosphor Persistence ----------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Parameter            | Current | Range         | Step   | Effect
  --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  phosphor_amplitude   |    0.02 | 0~0.2         | 0.01   | Phosphor persistence (trail); 0 = off
  phosphor_power       |    2.20 | 0.5~3.0       | 0.05   | Phosphor decay curve; larger = shorter
                                                            trail

-- Color -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Parameter            | Current | Range         | Step   | Effect
  --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  saturation           |    1.00 | 0~2           | 0.05   | Color saturation; 1.0 = unchanged
  NTSCintensity        |    0.80 | 0~1           | 0.1    | Blend toward NTSC -> sRGB color space
  BLK                  |    1.00 | -7.5~7.5      | 0.5    | Black level in IRE; positive lifts
                                                            blacks
  EXPOSURE             |     1.0 | 0.1~2.0       | 0.05   | Linear output gain (master brightness)
  CT                   |    1000 | -2800~2800    | 100    | Color temperature offset in K; positive
                                                            = cooler

-- Time --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Parameter            | Current | Range         | Step   | Effect
  --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  motion_blur          |     0.0 | 0~1           | 1      | 50/50 blend with previous frame; 1 = on

====================================================================================================
[4] Tuning Tips
====================================================================================================

  Goal                         | Action
  -----------------------------+---------------------------------------------------------
  Too bright / washed out      | raise monitorgamma to 2.4~2.6; lower halation and
                               | aperture_brightboost
  Too dark                     | lower monitorgamma; raise aperture_brightboost; EXPOSURE
                               | 1.1~1.2
  Colors look flat             | raise saturation to 1.2~1.4
  Highlights smear together    | lower halation below 0.05; lower width
  Scanlines too strong         | lower scanline_weight to 0.22~0.28
  Scanlines too faint          | raise scanline_weight to 0.35~0.45
  Dirty image with trails      | set phosphor_amplitude to 0
  Ghosting on moving objects   | motion_blur = 0; lower phosphor_amplitude
  Pixel edges too hard         | raise pixel_glow to 0.4~0.5
  Image too soft               | lower pixel_glow to 0.1~0.2
  Jagged rounded corners       | raise cornersmooth to 1600~2000
  Want a flat screen           | curvature = 0; cornersize = 0
  Mask invisible (high res)    | use a larger mask_type number (see section 5)
  Mask too coarse              | use a smaller mask_type number; lower aperture_strength

  Ready-made presets
  - Clean & sharp      aperture_strength 0.3, aperture_brightboost 0.5,
                       halation 0.05, scanline_weight 0.25, motion_blur 0
  - Warm home TV       keep this cgp as-is
  - Flat-panel look    curvature 0, cornersize 0, monitorgamma 2.2
  - Retro NTSC tape    NTSCintensity 0.8, BLK 1.0, CT 1000, saturation 1.05
  - No persistence     phosphor_amplitude 0

[5] mask_type Pattern Table (1~24)
====================================================================================================

  #   Pattern                             #   Pattern
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1   Aperture grille RGB (default)       13  delta 2x4x2 RGB
  2   2x2 shadow mask RGB                 14  Slot mask RGB (large holes)
  3   Slot mask RGB                       15  slot 2x4x4 RGB
  4   Aperture grille RBG                 16  Slot mask RBG
  5   2x2 shadow mask RBG                 17  slot 2x5x4 BGR
  6   Aperture grille 1/4 RGB (sparse)    18  slot 2x5x4 RGB
  7   Aperture grille 2/5 BGR             19  Aperture grille 2/5 BGR variant
  8   Aperture grille 3/6 RGB (dense)     20  Vertical stripe mask
  9   Aperture grille 2/4 RGB             21  HDR slot mask
  10  Aperture grille 3/6 RGB low TVL     22  Monochrome aperture grille
  11  delta 1x4 RGB                       23  Monochrome aperture grille 4K
  12  delta 2x4x1 RGB                     24  Shadow mask (6x10, coarsest)

  Rule: larger numbers generally mean coarser, sparser cells; the RBG
        suffix swaps B and R. Index 0 disables the mask and is outside
        the slider range (minimum is 1).

====================================================================================================
[7] Notes
====================================================================================================

  - The mask tiles against OUTPUT pixels, independent of zoom: patterns
    1~10 are clear at 1080p; use 14+ at 2160p.
  - R and d only take effect when curvature = 1.
  - phosphor_amplitude and motion_blur read past frames; FBNeo keeps about
    7 frames automatically.
  - Integer-step parameters (mask_type, curvature, interlace_detect,
    motion_blur) now round to the nearest step, so float error can no
    longer make a parameter look adjustable yet do nothing.
  - Curvature/corners affect only the screen edges, not the center.
